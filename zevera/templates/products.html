Get It Right The First Time

Measure Your Size At Home

No ring sizer? Use a strip of paper and a ruler — takes about two minutes.

01

Wrap a strip

Cut a thin strip of paper and wrap it snugly around the base of your finger.

02

Mark the overlap

Mark where the strip meets itself, then lay it flat against a ruler.

03

Measure in mm

That length is your finger's circumference — match it against our size scale above.

04
